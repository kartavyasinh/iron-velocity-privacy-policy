Iron Velocity Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · App: Iron Velocity (com.ironvelocity.game)

Iron Velocity is a single-player game. It has no accounts and no sign-in. This policy explains what information is handled when you play and why.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (missions completed, stars, cash and gold, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may serve ads from its mediation partners AppLovin and Unity Ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these services may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interactions.

Their use of this information is governed by their own policies: Google, AppLovin and Unity.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for it with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

United States privacy rights

We do not sell personal information and do not receive the data the ad services collect. Those services may use it for personalised advertising, which some US state laws treat as "sharing". To opt out, use Settings › Privacy › Consent where it is shown, or delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads), which turns off ad personalisation for every app.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only receives Google's confirmation that the purchase is owned, so it can turn ads off.

Rewarded ads are always optional. You never have to watch an ad to play or finish a mission.

Children

Iron Velocity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
